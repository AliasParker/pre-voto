# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

All participants in pre.voto — contributors, maintainers, and community members — are expected to treat each other with respect, act professionally, and foster an inclusive environment.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact [hola@pre.voto](mailto:hola@pre.voto). All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project leadership.
